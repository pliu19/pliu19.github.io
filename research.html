Research

My research bridges academic rigor and industrial scale. Currently, I focus on applying Graph Neural Networks and deep learning to large-scale recommendation and retrieval problems at LinkedIn.

Previously, my Ph.D. research focused on natural language processing in social networks, filter bubbles in news recommender systems, and active learning.

I focus specifically in these topics:

  • Recommender Systems — EBR (Embedding-based Retrieval), Ranking models, and Filter Bubbles
  • Graph Neural Networks (GNN) — Large-scale graph learning for industrial applications
  • Natural Language Processing — Hate speech detection and offensive language detection
  • Computer Vision — Microscopy image analysis and cell activity classification
