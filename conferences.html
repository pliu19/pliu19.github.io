Conference Deadlines

A working calendar of the venues I follow — information retrieval, recommender systems, data mining, and the general machine learning, AI, and NLP conferences that publish alongside them. It is scoped to what is useful for planning a submission year, not to be exhaustive.

Dates in plain text are confirmed against the official call for papers. Dates shown as like this are estimates projected from the same venue's previous cycle, because the call has not been posted yet — treat them as planning aids and confirm before you commit to one. All deadlines are AoE unless the venue says otherwise.

ACL Rolling Review Cycles

ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, and now COLING do not take direct submissions. You submit to an ARR cycle, receive reviews, and then commit the reviewed paper to a specific conference. The cycle deadline is the one that binds; the commitment deadline only matters once you are already in.

Cycle Submission Commitment Feeds into
Aug 2026 Aug 3, 2026 Oct 11, 2026 EACL 2027
Oct 2026 Oct 12, 2026 Dec 20, 2026 NAACL 2027, COLING 2027
Jan 2027 early Jan 2027 TBA ACL 2027
May 2027 late May 2027 TBA EMNLP 2027

ARR moved to ten-week cycles in May 2025, so there are five per year rather than six. Individual venues set their own commitment date within a few days of the cycle's — COLING 2027, for instance, lists December 23 against ARR's December 20.
